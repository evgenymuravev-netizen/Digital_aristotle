System Analyst Assessment — practice online

A knowledge check for systems, integration and technical analysts. It measures how well you reason about APIs & REST, protocols (SOAP, gRPC, GraphQL, WebSockets), the OSI model and networking, RabbitMQ & messaging, and databases. Start with a free full test, see exactly where you're strong and weak, then drill the ten timed forms.

📘 Study guide — the fundamentals

How it works

  • Free full test: 25 questions across all five domains in a full 15 minutes — no sign-up, not a cut-down sampler.
  • Ten more forms: 20 questions in 15 minutes each, graded easy → hard, spanning every domain.
  • Interleaved: question types are mixed so no two similar questions run back-to-back.
  • Adaptive: your results build a custom round from the topics you miss most.

All questions are original and vendor-neutral, written to reflect the everyday knowledge of a working systems analyst. For self-assessment and interview prep — not affiliated with any certification body.
