McQuaig-style Mental Agility Test — practice online

The Mental Agility Test measures how quickly and accurately you reason across verbal, numerical and logical problems. Start with a free 5-minute test, see exactly where you're strong and weak, then drill the ten full 15-minute forms.

📘 How to pass — strategy guide

How it works

  • Free taster: 12 mixed questions in 5 minutes — no sign-up.
  • Ten full forms: 25 harder questions in 15 minutes each, graded easy → hard.
  • Interleaved: question types are mixed so no two similar questions run back-to-back.
  • Adaptive: your results build a custom round from the topics you miss most.

Original practice items written in the spirit of the McQuaig Mental Agility Test. Not affiliated with, or endorsed by, The McQuaig Institute®. For self-practice only.
